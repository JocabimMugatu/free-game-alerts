Good to know

Questions, answered straight

Is it really free?
Yes. The free Wajo plan runs the alerts. No card, no trial that flips on you, no upsell emails.
Do I get to keep the games?
Most Epic giveaways are yours to keep forever if you claim them inside the window, which is exactly what the alert protects you from missing. Steam freebies are usually limited-time demos or promotions, and each alert tells you which.
What if a game shows up while I'm asleep?
Anything found during your quiet hours is batched into one morning message. Nothing quietly slips by, and nothing wakes you at 3 AM.
I use another AI assistant. Can I still get alerts?
Yes. The database is public and the six-step sync protocol lives in the repo README. Any assistant that can fetch a URL can follow it.
